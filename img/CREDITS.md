# Product photo credits

Every product photo in `img/parts/` is an official image published by the part's
maker, resized and cropped for this site. All rights belong to their owners:
AMD, Intel, NVIDIA, MSI, ASUS, Corsair, Kingston, Crucial (Micron), Sandisk (WD),
Samsung, Seagate, DeepCool, Thermalright, Noctua, ARCTIC, Seasonic, NZXT, Lian Li,
Fractal Design and Cooler Master.

They are used here only to identify the products in a non-commercial portfolio
project. If you run this site commercially, check each brand's media-use terms or
switch to images from the Amazon Product Advertising API.

Case colour variants (for example `4000d-white.webp`, `north-charcoal.webp`) come from
the same makers' product pages for that colour.

Notes: GeForce RTX 50-series cards share one official Founders Edition render,
the RTX 5060 included (NVIDIA makes no Founders Edition 5060). The Ryzen 5 7600 uses
AMD's Ryzen product image, and the Intel processors use Intel's official processor badges.
